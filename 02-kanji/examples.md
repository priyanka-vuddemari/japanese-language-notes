
## N5 Kanji Quiz Answers: Full Explanation

| Q | Japanese Sentence (Reading) | Romaji | English Translation | Target Kanji | Correct Answer (Hiragana) | Meaning & Explanation |

| 1 | あの 山 は おおきいです。 | Ano yama wa ookii desu. | That mountain is big. | 山 | やま | 山 (yama) is the kun'yomi (Japanese reading) for "mountain." This is the basic reading used when the kanji stands alone. |
| 2 | 川 の 水 は つめたいです。 | Kawa no mizu wa tsumetai desu. | The river's water is cold. | 川 | かわ | 川 (kawa) is the kun'yomi for "river." In JLPT N5, you encounter this reading frequently when talking about nature. |
| 3 | 田 なかさんは がくせいです。 | Tanaka-san wa gakusei desu. | Ms./Mr. Tanaka is a student. | 田 | たなか | 田 (ta) is the kun'yomi for "rice paddy." Combined in this surname, the reading remains ta. |
| 4 | きょうは いい 日 です。 | Kyō wa ii hi desu. | Today is a good day. | 日 | ひ | 日 (hi) is the kun'yomi for "sun" or "day." This reading is used when talking about a specific day or the physical sun. |
| 5 | 月 が きれいです。 | Tsuki ga kirei desu. | The moon is beautiful. | 月 | つき | 月 (tsuki) is the kun'yomi for "moon" or "month." This is the fundamental reading for the natural object in the sky. |
| 6 | 火 を つけないでください。 | Hi o tsukenai de kudasai. | Please don't light a fire. | 火 | ひ | 火 (hi) is the kun'yomi for "fire." This reading is used when referring to fire generally or an open flame. |
| 7 | 水 を ください。 | Mizu o kudasai. | Please give me water. | 水 | みず | 水 (mizu) is the kun'yomi for "water." This is how you ask for water to drink. |
| 8 | 木 が たくさん あります。 | Ki ga takusan arimasu. | There are many trees. | 木 | き | 木 (ki) is the kun'yomi for "tree" or "wood." This reading is used when talking about trees as physical objects. |
| 9 | 金 ようびに いきます。 | Kinyōbi ni ikimasu. | I will go on Friday. | 金 | きん | 金 (kin) is the on'yomi (Chinese reading) used in the word for Friday (金曜日, kinyōbi). |
| 10 | 土 ようびに あいましょう。 | Doyōbi ni aimashō. | Let's meet on Saturday. | 土 | ど | 土 (do) is the on'yomi used in the word for Saturday (土曜日, doyōbi). |
| 11 | 一 つ ください。 | Hitotsu kudasai. | Please give me one (thing). | 一 | ひと | 一 (hito) is the start of the counter hitotsu (one thing). This is the kun'yomi reading used for general counting of small items. |
| 12 | 二 人 で たべました。 | Futari de tabemashita. | Two people ate (together). | 二 | ふた | 二 (futa) is the start of the counter futari (two people). This specific word uses the kun'yomi reading. |

| 14 | 四 人 います。 | Yonin imasu. | There are four people. | 四 | よにん | 四 (yo) is the specific on'yomi used when counting people (yonin). |
| 15 | 五 つ あります。 | Itsutsu arimasu. | There are five (things). | 五 | いつ | 五 (itsu) is the start of the counter itsutsu (five things), using the kun'yomi. |
| 16 | 六 日 に いきます。 | Muika ni ikimasu. | I will go on the 6th (day). | 六 | むいか | 六 (mui) is part of the specific kun'yomi reading for the 6th day of the month (muika). |
| 17 | 七 つ たべました。 | Nanatsu tabemashita. | I ate seven (things). | 七 | なな | 七 (nana) is the start of the counter nanatsu (seven things), using the kun'yomi. |

| 19 | 九 つ ください。 | Kokonotsu kudasai. | Please give me nine (things). | 九 | ここ | 九 (koko) is the start of the counter kokonotsu (nine things), using the kun'yomi. |
